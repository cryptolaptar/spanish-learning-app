Palabra a Palabra

A Spanish vocabulary companion, A1 through C2

0%

Covers the eight tenses/moods used most in everyday Spanish speech and writing, deliberately leaving out literary-only forms (like the pretérito anterior or the future subjunctive). The pronunciation guide is a simplified respelling, not IPA: stressed syllables are shown in CAPS, "H" represents the throaty Spanish j/soft-g sound, and "NY" represents ñ.

Add this to your iPhone

This is a web app — once it's saved to your Home Screen it opens full-screen, just like a regular app, and works offline. Your progress is saved on your device.

  1. Open this file's URL in Safari on your iPhone (not Chrome — the "Add to Home Screen" option needs Safari).
  2. Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up), in the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right. An icon called "Palabra a Palabra" will appear on your Home Screen.
